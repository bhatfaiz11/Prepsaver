Privacy Policy

How PrepSaver collects, uses and protects your information when you use our website, WhatsApp support, and consultation services.

📅 Last Updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

PrepSaver ("we", "us", "our") is an independent medical education consultancy that helps NEET PG, FMGE, INI-CET and Residency aspirants find and enroll in courses on platforms like PrepLadder and Cerebellum, and access applicable coupon codes and cashback offers. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By using our website (prepsaver.in) or contacting us via WhatsApp, forms, or any other channel, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information when you interact with us:

  • Contact details you voluntarily share — such as your name, phone number, WhatsApp number, or email, when you fill our consultation form or message us directly.
  • Course preferences — such as which exam (NEET PG, FMGE, INI-CET, etc.) and which platform or course you're interested in.
  • Communication history — messages exchanged with us over WhatsApp or our consultation form, so we can assist you better.
  • Usage data — basic analytics about how visitors use our website (pages viewed, approximate location, device/browser type) collected automatically via Google Analytics.

We do not collect payment information — all course payments are made directly on the official PrepLadder or Cerebellum platforms, not through PrepSaver.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your queries and provide guidance on courses, pricing, and offers.
  • Apply and verify coupon codes and cashback eligibility on your behalf where applicable.
  • Notify you about relevant offers, such as early registration for upcoming sales, if you've opted in.
  • Improve our website content, course comparisons, and overall user experience.
  • Maintain records for support and dispute resolution purposes.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Third-Party Platforms & Links

Our website contains links and coupon redirects to third-party platforms such as PrepLadder and Cerebellum. Once you leave prepsaver.in and land on their site or app, their own privacy policies and terms apply — we encourage you to review those separately. PrepSaver is not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party platforms.

5. Cookies & Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site — such as which pages are most visited and how long users stay. This helps us improve our content and offers. Analytics data is aggregated and does not directly identify you. You can disable cookies through your browser settings at any time, though some site features may not function as intended.

6. WhatsApp & Direct Communication

If you contact us via WhatsApp or our consultation form, we use that information solely to respond to your query, share pricing/offer details, and follow up on your registration or purchase where relevant. We do not share your WhatsApp number with PrepLadder, Cerebellum, or any other third party without your consent.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you share with us from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for medical students and graduates preparing for postgraduate entrance exams. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 years of age.

9. Your Choices

  • You may request that we delete any personal information we hold about you by contacting us on WhatsApp.
  • You may opt out of promotional messages or offer notifications at any time by informing us directly.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
